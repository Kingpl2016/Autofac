Moving heads (target 'movers') get a position effect on their own; their color and intensity come from the light clips on 'all' or on their group.

| energy | position effect | period | size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| low | none, or panSweep / tiltSweep | '16' | 'small' |
| mid | circle, figure8 or panSweep | '8' | 'small' or 'medium' |
| high | fan, ballyhoo or waveFan | '4' | 'large' |

- Movement follows the music: change the movement where the section changes, not in the middle of a section.
- Do not move the heads during calm parts unless the user asks for movement.
- Use spread 'half' for a wave across the heads; mirror true for a symmetric two-wing look.
- Position effects only work on fixtures with pan/tilt; never put them on 'all' if the show has other fixtures too.
- Beam effects (zoomBreathe, irisPulse, goboSpin, prismSpin) are optional extras for high energy, only on 'movers'.
