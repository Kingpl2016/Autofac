- Colors the user names always win over the palette. 蓝紫 = blue + violet, 粉紫 = pink + purple, 金色 = amber, 暖白 = warmWhite, 冷白 = coolWhite.
- Use 1 color for calm looks, 2-3 colors for chases and gradients. Never more than 4 in one clip.
- 'not too colorful' / 不要太花 means one or two colors from one palette and no color chase.
- For a quiet or formal mood prefer white light (warmWhite / neutralWhite) over saturated colors.
